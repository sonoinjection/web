About SonoInjection

Why SonoInjection?

Ultrasound-guided injection procedures are skills that demand intensive hands-on experience and a sound technical approach beyond theoretical knowledge alone. SonoInjection offers participants the opportunity to work on cadaveric specimens, with the aim of safely developing ultrasonographic evaluation of anatomical structures, probe handling, needle visualization, and target-oriented injection techniques.

Our Training Approach

Our course programmes are designed around a highly interactive, practice-driven training model. Participants rotate through cadaver stations and receive one-on-one feedback from faculty during each application. The small-group format ensures that every participant gains active hands-on experience.

Certification

A certificate of attendance is presented to all participants at the end of the course. SonoInjection trainings are designed to support clinical practice and to develop current applied skills in musculoskeletal ultrasonography and interventional procedures.
